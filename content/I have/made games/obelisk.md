## About

Original idea of Obelisk is based upon the classic sliding puzzle game. The theme was `a Small world` this made me think about small but challenging games. And that brought me to a sliding puzzle. After that It was finding a way to use the principle of 9 block and use them differently. Eventually the idea of having a character that has to go from A to B via bridges between the blocks came to mind.

![level](obelisk.gif)

Every Chunk or block has half of a walk bridge. If the half aligns with another half they will lower and a character could walk over it. Some chunks could be swapped with eachother others could be rotated from a distance. There was also a possibility you had 2 characters you needed to get to the other side.

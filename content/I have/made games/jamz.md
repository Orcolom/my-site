[Dae research](http://www.digitalartsandentertainment.be/page/133/Research), requested the [team](#the-team) to create a prototype for one of their projects, [Iplay](http://www.digitalartsandentertainment.be/page/236/iPlay:%20Interactive%20sports%20platform). This setup has two screen (wall and floor) and full-body tracking. We created a rhythm game.

![conductor](jamz_conductor.png)

It was my task to find out how we would create the songs and let events go off when required. After doing some research in different tools and learning how beats and bars work. I concluded that we could do with the conductor that unity provided.

Unity's conductor has the option to link the update time to the audio time. This makes sure that notes showed up when they needed to be.

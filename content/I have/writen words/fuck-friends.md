date: 2022-02

Recognizable faces, recounting epic stories.\
Parties held together, by stamina long gone.\

Visits that should have been 15 minutes. 
but turned out to be 6 cities.\

Events a.p.a.r.t o.f

Fuck,\
I wish I\
had friends

<br>

Frozen in time,\
multiple tables, moments and lives,\ 
shapes ever evolving,\
just rearranged.

No matter

how close the bond is,\
always miles away

Fuck,\
I wish I\
got friends

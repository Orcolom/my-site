Over the years I have tried to find different way to play and broadcast weird and small games.

## Press any 🔑
Press any key was a game corner at a youth festival. The intention was to bring weird games into a space they normally don't exist. To support me in this I had build a tool that would not be able to provide access to the PC by any means necessary.\
https://www.instagram.com/p/CftMAudoLpt/

## Arium
Arium was an attempt at highlighting weird and wonderfull small video games that I would stumble upon in a digital space. I explored creating weekly Youtube videos and shorts.\
https://www.youtube.com/@arium_games/videos
